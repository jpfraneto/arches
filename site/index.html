Layer model

Small surface, clear boundary.

Hypersnap Lite is the write engine.

Arches consumes Hypersnap Lite through its Docker image and config contract.

Arches is the factory.

The installer generates the appliance files, domain wiring, API, web, feed, and admin scaffold.

Farcaster is the protocol.

Arches does not become a global Farcaster indexer. Each Arch feed is scoped to casts created through that Arch.
